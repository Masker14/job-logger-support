Common questions

Where are my jobs stored?
Only on your iPhone. Job Logger has no account and no server, so nobody else can see your jobs. They are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup).

If I delete the app, are my jobs deleted?
Yes. Deleting Job Logger deletes every job in it, and we cannot get them back for you. Your photos and videos are not affected: they stay in your Photos app, because Job Logger never moves or copies them. Send any jobs you want to keep before deleting the app.

Why are my videos not sent?
Videos are too large for email (Trello accepts at most 10 MB per email), so only photos are attached. Your videos stay in your Photos app, and the email says how many there were.

A photo is missing from a job.
Job Logger matches photos by the time they were taken, so a photo taken while the job was paused or stopped is not included. Use Add photos on the job to add it. If you gave Job Logger access to selected photos only, allow full access in the iPhone's Settings, then Job Logger, then Photos.

How do I send jobs to Trello?
In Trello, open your board's menu and choose Email-to-board. Copy that address into Send jobs to in Job Logger's Settings. Don't use Trello? Put in a regular email address instead.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Job Logger. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. The jobs you have already logged stay viewable, editable and sendable after you cancel.

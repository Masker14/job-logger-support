Privacy Policy

Job Logger for iPhone. Last updated 6 November 2026.

In short: Job Logger collects no data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap Send, and then it goes only to the email address you choose, from your own email account.

What the app stores, and where

Each job's address, customer name, visit times, notes, materials and location are stored on your iPhone only. There is no account, no server and no cloud copy run by us. Your jobs are included in your normal iPhone backup.

To count your free jobs, the app keeps a number in your iPhone's keychain, so reinstalling the app does not reset it. This number never leaves your iPhone.

Location

When you start a job, the app asks your iPhone for your location once, to suggest the job's address. The address is looked up using Apple's mapping service. The location is saved with the job on your iPhone only and is never included in the email. The app does not track your location at any other time.

Photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library to find the photos and videos taken while a job was running. It stores only a reference to each one, not a copy, and never changes or deletes anything in your library. Photos are attached to an email only when you tap Send. Videos are never sent.

Sending a job

When you tap Send, the app opens an email in your own Mail app (or the share sheet) with the job's details and photos, addressed to the address you set in Settings. You see the email before it goes, and it is sent by your email provider, not by us. If you send it to a Trello board, Trello's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared, because we receive none.

Deleting your data

Delete a job by swiping left on it in the list, or delete the app to remove every job. Your photos and videos stay in your Photos app either way. Emails you have already sent are kept by whoever received them.

Children

Job Logger is a work tool and is not directed at children.
